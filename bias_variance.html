Bias / Variance Tradeoff Study Notes

Summary of Concepts

  • Bias: Error due to oversimplifying a model. High bias = Underfitting (ignoring data patterns).
  • Variance: Error due to sensitivity to training noise. High variance = Overfitting (memorizing noise).
  • MSE Decomposition: MSE = Bias² + Variance + Irreducible Error. To minimize total error, we must balance bias and variance.
  • Relation to MAP: Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) minimizes training error but can lead to high variance. Maximum A Posteriori (MAP) introduces a prior distribution (like assuming weights should be near zero). This acts as regularization (L1/L2), deliberately increasing Bias slightly to achieve a massive reduction in Variance, yielding a better generalizing model.
