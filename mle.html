Maximum Likelihood Estimation

Interactive Univariate Gaussian Fitter

How this works

Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) is a method for estimating the parameters of a statistical model given observations. In this interactive, you are looking at a fixed dataset of 1D points (the dots on the axis). We assume this data was generated by a Normal (Gaussian) Distribution, which is defined by two parameters: the Mean (μ) and the Standard Deviation (σ).

Your Goal: Use the sliders to adjust the curve so it best "fits" the data. As you move the sliders, watch the Log-Likelihood score. MLE states that the best parameters are the ones that maximize this score. When you find the peak value, you have found the Maximum Likelihood Estimate!
