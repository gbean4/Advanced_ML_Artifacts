Forward KL: DKL(p || q)

  • Also known as: Inclusive KL, Moment Projection.
  • Behavior: Zero-avoiding / Mode-covering.
  • Intuition: Penalizes the model q infinitely if it misses any part of the true data p. Forces q to stretch out and cover all peaks, often overestimating the data's spread.

Reverse KL: DKL(q || p)

  • Also known as: Exclusive KL, Information Projection.
  • Behavior: Zero-forcing / Mode-seeking.
  • Intuition: Penalizes the model q infinitely if it places probability mass where the true data p is empty. Forces q to lock onto a single peak, often underestimating the data's spread.
