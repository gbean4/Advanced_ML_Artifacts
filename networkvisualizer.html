Ranking Loss Visualizer

This interactive artifact helps you understand how Siamese and Triplet networks learn to group similar items and push apart dissimilar ones.

1. The Intuition

Imagine a facial recognition system. It doesn't classify identities; it measures similarity. We penalize the model (apply a "loss") if it thinks two photos of you are very different, or if it thinks a photo of you and a stranger are very similar.

2. Network Architectures

  • Siamese (Pairwise): Looks at 2 examples at a time. Forces "positive pairs" to be close and "negative pairs" to be pushed apart by a minimum margin.
  • Triplet: Looks at 3 examples (Anchor, Positive, Negative). Adjusts so the Anchor is always closer to the Positive than the Negative by a margin.

3. The Math & Interaction

How to use: Select a mode below and drag the points on the canvas (representing the "embedding space"). Watch the loss update.

Siamese Mode: Notice how pulling a positive pair apart increases loss, while pulling a negative pair apart reduces loss until it hits the margin m.

Triplet Mode: Drag the Negative point closer to the Anchor than the Positive point. Watch the loss shoot up because the model is failing to keep the similar items closest!
