2 Generative vs. Discriminative

Explore the theoretical trade-offs. Generative models (like Naive Bayes) model the joint distribution $P(X,Y)$. Discriminative models (like Logistic Regression) model the conditional distribution $P(Y|X)$ directly.

The Artist Analogy

Generative: Learns the fundamental rules of *how* Monet and Picasso paint, then asks "Could Monet have painted this?".
Discriminative: Ignores overall style and just learns the boundary: "Monet uses pastels, Picasso uses geometry."
